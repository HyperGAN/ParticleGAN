Scientific Scope & Calibration Caveats

Counts describe screening and selection evidence only. Scientific superiority or public-default adoption requires report-bound accepted calibration and independently frozen confirmation evidence; software repairs grant neither, and unmeasured confirmation remains unmeasured.

  • Unequal tuning budgets: Rows with different registered searches or candidate card counts are not equally tuned comparisons.
  • Distinct non-pass outcomes: not_run, blocked, incompatible, and error outcomes are never collapsed into numerical FAIL and earn no pass credit.
  • Ordinary vs. diagnostic scope: Assisted, recipe-adapted, or separate diagnostic runs remain archived under their original identity and never replace an ordinary selected-cohort grade.
