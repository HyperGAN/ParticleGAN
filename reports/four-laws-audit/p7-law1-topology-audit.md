## 1. Rigorous Topological vs. Quantitative Approximation Formalization

### 1.1 Theorem 1 — Exact Topological Support Obstruction (and Its Strict Scope Boundary)

**Theorem 1 (Connected Pushforward Support Obstruction & Infinite Reverse-KL):**
Let $Z$ be a connected topological space equipped with a Borel probability measure $P_Z$ of full topological support $\operatorname{supp}(P_Z) = Z$ (e.g., $Z = \mathbb{R}^{d_z}$ with $P_Z = \mathcal{N}(0, I_{d_z})$), and let $G: Z \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be continuous. Then:
1. **Connected Pushforward Support:** $\operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z) = \overline{G(Z)}$, which is **connected**.
   - *Proof:* Because $\mathbb{R}^d \setminus \overline{G(Z)}$ is open and disjoint from $G(Z)$, its preimage under $G$ is empty, so $(G_\# P_Z)(\mathbb{R}^d \setminus \overline{G(Z)}) = 0$, implying $\operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z) \subseteq \overline{G(Z)}$. Conversely, for any $y = G(z_0) \in G(Z)$ and open $U \ni y$, continuity makes $G^{-1}(U)$ an open neighborhood of $z_0 \in \operatorname{supp}(P_Z)$, so $(G_\# P_Z)(U) = P_Z(G^{-1}(U)) > 0$; thus $G(Z) \subseteq \operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z)$, and taking closures gives $\overline{G(Z)} \subseteq \operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z)$. Since $Z$ is connected and $G$ is continuous, $G(Z)$ and its closure $\overline{G(Z)}$ are connected. $\square$
2. **Exact Support Impossibility:** If $\operatorname{supp}(P_Y) = \bigcup_{m=1}^M K_m$ ($M \ge 2$) is a finite union of nonempty compact sets with pairwise separation $\Delta := \min_{i \neq j} \operatorname{dist}(K_i, K_j) > 0$, then $\operatorname{supp}(P_Y)$ is disconnected; hence $\operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z) \neq \operatorname{supp}(P_Y)$ and **$G_\# P_Z \neq P_Y$**.
3. **Positive Valley Mass & Infinite Reverse-KL:** Whenever $G(Z)$ intersects at least two components $K_i, K_j$ ($i \neq j$), connectedness of $G(Z)$ forces $G(Z)$ to intersect the open valley $V := \mathbb{R}^d \setminus \bigcup_{m=1}^M K_m$. Since $G^{-1}(V)$ is nonempty and open in $Z = \operatorname{supp}(P_Z)$, $(G_\# P_Z)(V) > 0 = P_Y(V)$. Hence $G_\# P_Z \not\ll P_Y$ and **$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(G_\# P_Z \| P_Y) = +\infty$ identically** for every mode-covering continuous $G$.

> **Critical Scope Boundary (Strictly Disconnected Supports vs. Gaussian Mixtures):**
> Every Gaussian mixture $P_Y = \sum_{m=1}^M w_m \mathcal{N}(\mu_m, \sigma_m^2 I_d)$ — including ParticleGAN's flagship Law-1 benchmarks `grid100`, `rotated100`, `staggered100`, `ring16`, and `vector_*` — has strictly positive density everywhere, so **$\operatorname{supp}(P_Y) = \mathbb{R}^d$ is CONNECTED**. Theorem 1 does **NOT** forbid exact pushforward equality on Gaussian mixtures: in 1D, the quantile map $G(z) = F_Y^{-1}(\Phi(z))$ is $C^\infty$ and achieves $G_\# \mathcal{N}(0, 1) = P_Y$ identically (and smooth Brenier/Knothe–Rosenblatt diffeomorphisms exist in any $\mathbb{R}^d$). Instead, Gaussian mixtures exhibit an **extreme quantitative Jacobian cliff**: for $P_Y = \frac{1}{2}\mathcal{N}(-c, \sigma^2) + \frac{1}{2}\mathcal{N}(+c, \sigma^2)$, the exact $C^\infty$ Brenier map satisfies $G'(0) = \phi(0)/p_Y(0) = \sigma \exp\!\big(c^2/(2\sigma^2)\big)$. On adjacent `grid100` modes ($2c = 1.0, \sigma = 0.03$, $33.3\sigma$ separation), the midpoint density is $10^{-59.19}$ and **$G'(0) = 6.25 \times 10^{58}$** (Exp 1B table below). Only genuinely disconnected compact supports (e.g., discrete `img_bars4` or truncated disjoint balls) invoke Theorem 1's strict topological impossibility.

### 1.3 Theorem 3 — Exact Sharp Quantitative Trade-Offs (Gaussian Isoperimetry, Multi-Mode Lattices & `grid100`)

**Theorem 3 (Sharp Dimension-Free Borell–Sudakov–Tsirelson Lower Bounds on $p_{\text{bridge}}$, $W_1$, $W_2$, and $\|J_G\|_\infty$):**
Let $Z \sim \gamma_{d_z} = \mathcal{N}(0, I_{d_z})$ ($d_z \ge 1$) and let $G: \mathbb{R}^{d_z} \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be $L$-Lipschitz ($L = \sup_z \|J_G(z)\|_2$ for $C^1$ maps).

1. **Equal-Mass Bimodal Sharp Bound (Any $d_z \ge 1$, Tanielian et al. ICML 2020):**
   Let $K_1, K_2 \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ satisfy $\operatorname{dist}(K_1, K_2) = \Delta > 0$, $A_i = G^{-1}(K_i)$ with equal mass $\gamma_{d_z}(A_1) = \gamma_{d_z}(A_2) = \frac{1 - p_{\text{bridge}}}{2}$. Since $\operatorname{dist}(A_1, A_2) \ge r := \Delta/L$, Borell's Gaussian isoperimetric inequality $\gamma_{d_z}(A_1^r) \ge \Phi(\Phi^{-1}(\gamma_{d_z}(A_1)) + r)$ yields $1 - \gamma_{d_z}(A_2) \ge \Phi(\Phi^{-1}(\gamma_{d_z}(A_1)) + \Delta/L)$, hence:
   $$p_{\text{bridge}} \;\ge\; 2\Phi\!\left(\frac{\Delta}{2L}\right) - 1 = \operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{\Delta}{2\sqrt{2}\,L}\right) \iff L \;\ge\; \frac{\Delta}{2\,\Phi^{-1}\!\left(\frac{1 + \alpha}{2}\right)} = \frac{\Delta}{2\sqrt{2}\,\operatorname{erf}^{-1}(\alpha)} \;\sim\; \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\alpha} \quad (\alpha \to 0^+).$$
   Equality holds in every dimension $d_z \ge 1$ for parallel half-spaces $\{z_1 \le -\delta\}$, $\{z_1 \ge +\delta\}$.

2. **Exact Sharp $W_1$ and $W_2$ Lower Bounds (Any $d_z \ge 1, d \ge 1$, Attained by Ramp $G_{\Delta/(2L)}$):**
   For any $P_Y$ with $P_Y(K_1) = P_Y(K_2) = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\operatorname{dist}(K_1, K_2) = \Delta$, the map $h(x) := \frac{1}{2}(\operatorname{dist}(x, K_1) - \operatorname{dist}(x, K_2))$ is $1$-Lipschitz with $h|_{K_1} \le -\Delta/2$ and $h|_{K_2} \ge +\Delta/2$. Then $u := h \circ G$ is $L$-Lipschitz on $(\mathbb{R}^{d_z}, \gamma_{d_z})$. Letting $m$ be a median of $u(Z)$, Borell isoperimetry on $\{u \le m\}$ and $\{u \ge m\}$ gives $Q_u(p) \le m + L\Phi^{-1}(p)$ and $Q_u(1-p) \ge m - L\Phi^{-1}(p)$ for all $p \in (\frac{1}{2}, 1)$, hence $Q_u(p) - Q_u(1-p) \le 2L\Phi^{-1}(p)$. By $1$-Lipschitz contraction and 1D quantile $W_1$/$W_2$ identities (substituting $s = \Phi^{-1}(p)$, $\delta_0 := \Delta/(2L)$):
   $$W_1(G_\# \gamma_{d_z}, P_Y) \;\ge\; \int_0^{\delta_0} (\Delta - 2Ls)\phi(s)\,ds = \Delta\!\left(\Phi(\delta_0) - \frac{1}{2}\right) - 2L\big(\phi(0) - \phi(\delta_0)\big) \;\sim\; \frac{\Delta^2}{4\sqrt{2\pi}\,L},$$
   $$W_2^2(G_\# \gamma_{d_z}, P_Y) \;\ge\; \frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\delta_0} (\Delta - 2Ls)^2\phi(s)\,ds = \left(\frac{\Delta^2}{2} + 2L^2\right)\!\left(\Phi(\delta_0) - \frac{1}{2}\right) - 2\Delta L\phi(0) + \Delta L\phi(\delta_0) \;\sim\; \frac{\Delta^3}{12\sqrt{2\pi}\,L}.$$
   Therefore $W_1 \le \varepsilon \implies L = \Omega(\Delta^2/\varepsilon)$ and $W_2 \le \varepsilon \implies L = \Omega(\Delta^3/\varepsilon^2)$ (quantifying Cornish et al., ICML 2020 without requiring bijectivity!). Both bounds are **attained with exact equality** by the constructive ramp $G_{\Delta/(2L)}(z_1)$!

3. **Multi-Mode $M$-Component Generalization (Any $d_z \ge 1$) & Macroscopic Poincaré Bound:**
   For $M \ge 2$ disjoint components $K_1, \dots, K_M$ with $\min_{i \neq j}\operatorname{dist}(K_i, K_j) \ge \Delta > 0$ and preimage masses $p_m = \gamma_{d_z}(A_m)$ ($\sum_m p_m = 1 - \alpha$), the open half-radius enlargements $A_m^{r/2}$ ($r = \Delta/L$) are **pairwise disjoint** by the triangle inequality, and $A_m^{r/2} \setminus A_m \subseteq G^{-1}(V)$. Summing Borell's inequality across all $M$ cells yields:

1. **Precise formulation:** For connected $Z = \operatorname{supp}(P_Z)$ and continuous $G: Z \to \mathbb{R}^d$, $\operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z) = \overline{G(Z)}$ is connected, so $G_\# P_Z \neq P_Y$ and $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(G_\# P_Z \| P_Y) = +\infty$ for disconnected compact $\operatorname{supp}(P_Y)$. Quantitatively, for $P_Z = \mathcal{N}(0, I_{d_z})$ and $M$ modes separated by $\Delta > 0$, pushing bridge mass $\le \alpha$ requires $L = \|J_G\|_\infty \ge \frac{\Delta}{2[\Phi^{-1}(1/M) - \Phi^{-1}((1-\alpha)/M)]} = \Omega(\frac{\Delta\sqrt{\ln M}}{\alpha})$, and achieving $W_1 \le \varepsilon$ or $W_2 \le \varepsilon$ on equal-mass bimodal targets requires $L = \Omega(\Delta^2/\varepsilon)$ and $L = \Omega(\Delta^3/\varepsilon^2)$ respectively.
2. **Assumptions:** Connected full-support latent law (standard Gaussian for quantitative bounds), continuous / $L$-Lipschitz $G$, separated mode sets $\operatorname{dist}(K_i, K_j) \ge \Delta > 0$ carrying balanced mass $p_m = (1-\alpha)/M$.

4. **Potential counterexamples:** (a) **Practical metric approximation is NOT impossible:** continuous maps achieve arbitrarily small $W_1, W_2, \mathrm{TV}, \mathrm{MMD}$, and $p_{\text{bridge}} = \alpha > 0$ if $\|J_G\|_\infty = \Theta(\Delta/\alpha)$ is permitted (Table 1.1); (b) **Gaussian mixtures have connected support $\mathbb{R}^d$:** exact $C^\infty$ pushforwards exist, though with astronomically steep Brenier slopes (`6.25e58` on `grid100` adjacent modes); (c) **Discontinuous/stochastic generators** (e.g., rejection sampling, discrete latent gating, diffusion/flow ODEs with score blowup) bypass continuity.

> Let $G_\theta: Z \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be a continuous neural pushforward acting on a connected full-support prior $P_Z$ (e.g., $\mathcal{N}(0, I_{d_z})$ on $\mathbb{R}^{d_z}$), and let $P_Y = \sum_{m=1}^M w_m P_m$ be a multi-modal target whose mode cores $K_1, \dots, K_M$ have pairwise separation $\Delta = \min_{i \neq j}\operatorname{dist}(K_i, K_j) > 0$:
> 1. **Exact Topological Obstruction vs. Metric Approximation:** Because $\operatorname{supp}(G_\# P_Z) = \overline{G(Z)}$ is connected, exact support equality ($G_\# P_Z = P_Y$) is topologically impossible and $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(G_\# P_Z \| P_Y) = +\infty$ whenever $\operatorname{supp}(P_Y)$ is compact and disconnected. By contrast, continuous pushforwards *can* approximate $P_Y$ arbitrarily well in $W_1, W_2, \mathrm{TV}, \mathrm{MMD}$ and valley bridge mass $p_{\text{bridge}} \le \alpha$ — and exact $C^\infty$ pushforwards *do* exist for connected-support Gaussian mixtures — **at an unavoidable quantitative Jacobian cost** governed by Gaussian isoperimetry (Borell–Sudakov–Tsirelson): for any $L$-Lipschitz $G$ on $\mathcal{N}(0, I_{d_z})$ ($d_z \ge 1$) achieving balanced mode mass $(1-\alpha)/M$ and bridge mass $\le \alpha$,
>    $$\|J_G\|_\infty \;\ge\; \frac{\Delta}{2\big(\Phi^{-1}(1/M) - \Phi^{-1}((1-\alpha)/M)\big)} \;\sim\; \frac{\sqrt{2\ln M}}{2\ln\!\left(\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\right)}\,\Delta \;=\; \Omega\!\left(\frac{\Delta\sqrt{\ln M}}{\alpha}\right),$$
>    and on equal-mass bimodal targets, $W_1 \le \varepsilon \implies \|J_G\|_\infty = \Omega(\Delta^2/\varepsilon)$ and $W_2 \le \varepsilon \implies \|J_G\|_\infty = \Omega(\Delta^3/\varepsilon^2)$ (independent of adversarial training).
> 2. **Division of Labor & Finite-Batch Mass Integrity:** Because this obstruction is purely geometric (holding identically under non-adversarial OT objectives), decoupling *where* mass resides (an adaptive, topologically compatible multimodal prior — not merely a fixed mismatched mixture) from *how* it is locally shaped (a smooth low-Lipschitz chart $G_\theta$ with $\|J_G\| = O(\max_m \sigma_m/\sigma_z)$) eliminates both valley ghost mass and the Jacobian cliff. When $B < M$ or mode masses are unequal, persistent streaming prototype/chart statistics with mass-balanced hard assignment (preventing cross-valley barycentric averaging) and local scale/covariance tracking (preserving anisotropy when present) are required without a step clock.
