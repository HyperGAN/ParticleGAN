## 6. Conceptual Separation: Open Research Selection vs. Future Post-Freeze Validation

A core governance lesson of this audit ([P8](p8-qualification-audit.md)) is that **open discovery** and **confirmatory validation** serve opposite statistical functions and fail whenever conflated — yet separating them **does NOT require freezing Forge today**:

| Dimension | Stage A — Open Evolving Forge Discovery *(Active Now)* | Stage B — Future Post-Freeze Validation Concept *(e.g., `FUSQP-v1` Blueprint — NOT Launched Here)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Status Today** | **Open, live, continuously evolving** (add new hard tests, observables, and falsification probes freely — never freeze). | **Design concept only** — invoked *only* when a candidate family is deliberately frozen to claim scientific superiority or default adoption. |
| **Primary Scientific Goal** | Falsify hypotheses, isolate causal mechanisms, diagnose failure modes, and explore new algorithm families rapidly. | Quantify unbiased out-of-distribution generalization, cross-seed reliability, and true `J-to-Q` superiority with zero adaptive selection bias. |
| **Seed & Task Policy** | Protocol `seed = 0` (`AGENTS.md`: no seed-only score shopping), open task pool expanding with revised theory (Section 3 above). | Cryptographic lock of `(recipe_sha256, source_commit, git_tree_sha256)` prior to a single execution over disjoint unseen tasks (Pillars A–C: held-out architectures, mathematical invariants, independent pathological & real-image FID distributions) and disjoint confirmatory seeds (reporting full distributions & exact Clopper–Pearson 95% CIs, never selecting a seed). |
| **Allowed Claims** | Hypothesis support, controlled same-cohort ablations, existential witnesses, and honest failure discovery. | Confirmatory generalization claims, non-inferiority bounds, and (only with accepted calibration) `default_adoption: true`. |
| **Interaction Rule** | Any task discovered to expose a new law failure enters Stage A permanently; its receipts remain immutable. | Any post-hoc tweak after viewing Stage B results invalidates that validation cohort and returns the candidate to Stage A (requiring a fresh unseen partition for any future freeze). |
