## 1. What Do We Now Know?

Across 10 domain audit tracks (**P0–P10**), `15` formal theorem/impossibility blocks (`T1–T15` in [`p10-synthesis-and-ledger.md`](p10-synthesis-and-ledger.md#41-proven-theorems--impossibility-results-established-across-p0p9)), `90+` automated proof/regression tests in [`tests/four_laws_audit/`](../../tests/four_laws_audit/), and controlled RTX A6000 / CPU experiments, we have established **five mathematically irreducible obstructions** and **six verified causal mechanisms** that survive every adversarial challenge:

1. **Law 1 — Representational Geometry Is a Sharp, GAN-Independent Isoperimetric Trade-Off ([P7](p7-law1-topology-audit.md)):**
   - Pushing a connected full-support prior $P_Z$ through continuous $G_\theta$ onto compact disconnected supports $\operatorname{supp}(P_Y) = \bigcup_{m=1}^M K_m$ makes exact support matching impossible ($D_{\mathrm{KL}}(G_\# P_Z \,\|\, P_Y) = +\infty$).
   - Metric approximation ($W_1, W_2, \mathrm{TV}, \mathrm{MMD}, p_{\text{bridge}} \le \alpha$) and exact $C^\infty$ pushforwards on connected-support Gaussian mixtures (`grid100`) *do* exist, but obey sharp, dimension-free Borell–Sudakov–Tsirelson lower bounds ($\lVert J_G \rVert_\infty \ge \Delta/[2(\Phi^{-1}(1/M)-\Phi^{-1}((1-\alpha)/M))]$, forcing $L \ge 104.82$ at $\alpha = 0.01$ on `grid100` and exact 1D Brenier slope $6.25\times 10^{58}$).
   - **Zero-discriminator verification:** Decoupling an adaptive multimodal prior from a tiny `82`-parameter smooth generator crushes a `33,666`-parameter unimodal generator by **`111×` in valley ghost mass** (`82.1% -> 0.74%`) and **`9.2×` in max Jacobian norm** under pure Sinkhorn divergence $S_\varepsilon$ — while fixed mismatched multimodal priors make the cliff *worse* (`94.3%`).
2. **Law 2 — Mixed-Derivative Geometry, Cheap-Gradient Crossover & Dual Role of Critic Penalties ([P3](p3-game-dynamics-counterexample.md), [P6](p6-law2-autodiff-audit.md)):**
   - Differentiating any input-gradient penalty $\Phi(\lVert\nabla_x D_\psi(x)\rVert_2^2)$ w.r.t. $\psi$ necessarily computes exact **mixed second-order derivatives** $\frac{\partial^2 D_\psi}{\partial \psi \partial x} \nabla_x D_\psi$ (nonzero even on ReLU/LeakyReLU networks).
   - Evaluating these via **reverse-over-forward (RoF, `fwAD.dual_level` + `.backward()`)** avoids activation kink impulses (`0` flips, `grad_cos = 0.987–0.996`) and cuts `256²` BF16 `DCGANCritic` penalty latency **`3.64×`**, peak VRAM **`-33.1%`**, and NVML GPU energy **`-66.4%`** vs. reverse-over-reverse (RoR) — **specifically** in the compute-bound high-res Conv2d ($\ge 128^2, K \in \{1,2\}$) regime.
   - Critic slope penalties serve **two distinct roles**: Law 2's spatial force-field regularity ($\lVert\nabla_x D\rVert \le \kappa$) AND Law 4.5's equilibrium normal-direction dissipation ($\mu_D = \lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2_{\psi\psi}\mathcal{R}\big\rvert_{D=0,\ker(\Sigma_Y)}) > 0$).
3. **Law 3 — Two Mathematically Orthogonal Transport Obstructions ([P2](p2-law3-identifiability.md)):**

4. **Law 4 — Five Rigorously Sharpened Dynamical Principles ([P0](p0-law43-rank-audit.md), [P1](p1-law42-scale-settling.md), [P3](p3-game-dynamics-counterexample.md), [P4](p4-function-space-generality.md), [P5](p5-optimizer-asymmetry.md)):**
   - **4.1 Realization & 4.3 Update Control:** Player capacity mismatch $\rho \gg 1$ causes Kreiss non-normal transients $\sup_k \lVert M^k\rVert_2 = \Theta(\rho)$ (`381.4×` at $\rho=500$) even at $\rho(M)<1$; realized nonlinear probing catches $\Gamma \gg 1$ jumps (`2,309×–96,399×`) missed by JVP and bounds $\Theta(\gamma^5)$ external gain where LARS/Adam/Muon diverge linearly, while `LARS` (not global scalar trust) is required for internal homogeneous gauge $\alpha^0$.
