### 4.2 Detailed Five-Part Dissection of Every Major Mechanism

#### Mechanism 1 — Fused `Z = [X, Y]` 5-Step Quintic Newton–Schulz Polar Orthogonalization
*Modules:* [`_unified_v11/optimizer.py:70–245`](../../particlegan/_unified_v11/optimizer.py#L70-L245), [`_unified_v12/optimizer.py:456–644,928–946`](../../particlegan/_unified_v12/optimizer.py#L456-L644), [`_unified_v4/optimizer.py:24–30`](../../particlegan/_unified_v4/optimizer.py#L24-L30), [`_t4_arch/optimizer.py:34–42`](../../particlegan/_t4_arch/optimizer.py#L34-L42).
- **(a) Fundamental Problem(s) Addressed (Multi-Law Roles):** Primarily addresses **Law 4.2 (Spectral Conditioning)** by lifting collapsed subdominant singular values ($\sigma_i / \lVert G \rVert_F \gtrsim 10^{-3}$) in rotated singular bases along their true singular vectors $(u_i, v_i)$ with zero off-basis distortion, decoupling operator norm from Frobenius norm to break the Irreducible Frobenius–Spectral Dilemma. Secondarily interacts with **Law 4.4** (via Tikhonov damping $Y = \beta I_m$ scaled by `s_eff`) and **Law 4.5** (unnecessary Critic NS injects `2.9×–7.8×` spatial force-field jitter $\Delta(\nabla_x D)$ relative to logits $\Delta D$).
- **(b) Supporting Evidence:** Round-4 Arm 1 (`readout-690dd40a`, `readout-0219c6e2`) & `minimal-v2` (`readout-6bac8895`) prove 2D NS is strictly required for 16L skip-free `grid100_mlp16` (`prec 0.9929 -> 0.0244` without it) while 100% redundant on shallow/residual/BN-Conv2d layers; [P0](p0-law43-rank-audit.md) verifies `92.4%` true tail energy (`0%` off-basis) vs. `1.5%–6.1%` (`45%–74%` off-basis) for Adam/SignSGD; [P5](p5-optimizer-asymmetry.md) proves 5-step Critic NS is equally required when 16L skip-free pathology is placed on $D$ (`Regime B1a` `hq 0.286 -> 0.795/0.906`; standalone `0.640 -> 1.000`; `t3_iso` `0.3885 -> 0.5859`).
- **(c) Demonstrated Failure Modes & Code Defects:** (1) Finite 5-step small-SV gain ceiling $\prod a_k = 492.75$ cannot lift $\sigma_2/\sigma_1 \le 10^{-6}$ in exact arithmetic; (2) **BF16 rounding-noise leakage** at `s = 0` (`_unified_v11/optimizer.py:133` for $m \ge 64$, `_t4_arch/optimizer.py:42` for $m \ge 32$) injects `77.8%–86.8%` orthogonal subspace noise on rank-1 inputs (`62.0%` on `grid100_mlp16` `net.16`); (3) **rank-1 spectral norm blowup** (`_unified_v11/optimizer.py:143–150`) rescales to `target_frob = sqrt(m)`, inflating $\sigma_1 \to 11.31$ on $128\times 128$; (4) syntactic `_generator_free = (total_gen_mats == 0)` dispatch (`recipes.py:2381–2388`, pre-#644 `2378–2385`) disables Critic NS on deep skip-free critics whenever any trainable generator matrix exists; (5) stale docstrings (`BF16 65..128` vs. FP32 `m<=256`; false `+ Schatten-6` in `_unified_v12/optimizer.py:457`).
- **(d) Computational & Energy Expense:** 5 matrix multiplications per matrix ($O(m^2 n)$ per layer). Dropping redundant 4D Conv2d NS collapsed `opt_d + opt_g` on `r256` **`16.2×`** (`39.81 ms [51.0%] -> 2.45 ms [6.1%]`, cutting total energy `18.86 -> 11.20 J/st`, [P9 Sec. 4.2](p9-receipts-efficiency-audit.md)). Keeping 2D G-only NS on `grid100_mlp16` costs only `+6.2%` latency over zero-NS `minimal` (`63.11` vs. `59.43 ms/st`, `9.11` vs. `7.24 J/st`) while saving `-19.5%` latency / `-16.3%` energy vs. `v11` G+D NS.
- **(e) Alternative Mechanisms for a Brand-New Algorithm Family:** (i) **Compound pathology-triggered polar/NS** (gating NS on measured severe collapse $r_{\text{stable}} < 1.05$ + depth-skew/contraction witness rather than player role); (ii) **1-step or amortized Kronecker Shampoo / K-FAC** ($L G R$ with periodic root-inverse refresh, algebraically equivalent to Tikhonov polar at step 1); (iii) **Architectural prevention** (residual skips, RMSNorm/LayerNorm, orthogonal/spectral-preserving blocks, or muP scaling that keep $r_{\text{stable}} \gg 1$ so zero-NS `Frob-MP` suffices without matrix iterations); (iv) **Low-rank randomized spectral sketching / Lanczos top-$k$ deflation** for very wide layers.
