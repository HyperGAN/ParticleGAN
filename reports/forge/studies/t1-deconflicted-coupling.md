## 2. First-Principles Formulation

### 2.1 Why Continuous Generators Blur or Merge Disconnected Modes
Given a continuous map $G_\theta(z)$ pushing forward a smooth prior $P_Z$ toward a disconnected multi-modal law $P_Y = \sum_{m=1}^M w_m P_m$, standard minibatch OT and relativistic critic couplings fail along three distinct axes:

1. **Cross-Valley Barycentric Shrinkage & $B_r < M$ Mode Starvation**: Soft Sinkhorn couplings $\Pi_{ij}$ split mass across distinct modes for latent samples straddling decision boundaries, pulling $G_\theta(z_i)$ toward empty midpoints $\sum_j \Pi_{ij} y_j$ (`img_bars4` 0.5-ghost superpositions). Scalar centered log-odds $\tilde{D}(x) = D(x) - \bar{D}$ carries only 1 degree of freedom: it uniquely balances $K \le 2$ modes (`two_pole`, `img_stripes2`), but cannot resolve multi-way mode imbalances for $K \ge 3$. Moreover, when $B_r < M$ (`grid100` with $B_r = 64 < M = 100$), at least $\approx e^{-B_r/M} M \approx 53$ modes are absent in every minibatch, so minibatch slack couplings falsely kick already-settled samples off valid modes.
2. **Global Horizon Scale Conflation vs. Weibull Tail Inflation**: A single global scale $\ell_t \propto Q_{0.25}(d_Y^{\text{NN}})$ either blurs narrow modes ($\sigma = 0.05$) when dominated by wide modes ($\sigma = 0.35$) on `vector_unequal_width`, or — if replaced by naive pointwise $k$-NN distances — inflates $\ell$ by $Q_{90}/Q_{25} \approx 2.8\times$ in the outer tails of homogeneous Gaussians, degrading `two_pole` slope control.
3. **Dense Layer Activation-Gram Interference & Conditioned Drift Saturation**: For a shared linear layer $y_i = W h_i + b$, post-LeakyReLU features $h_i$ share a dominant common mean/correlated subspace $\Sigma_H = \frac{1}{B}\sum_i h_i h_i^\top$, causing opposing mode displacements $\delta_i$ to destructively interfere in $G_W = \sum_i \delta_i h_i^\top$. On continuous conditioned fibers $(c, x)$, large context drift $B c$ saturates fixed-bandwidth joint kernels before within-fiber innovation covariance $\Sigma_{x|c}$ is resolved.

### 2.2 Track 1B Deconflicted Piecewise Coupling (`T1CouplingLoss`)

1. **Persistent Streaming Chart Atlas ($K_{\max}=256$) & Screened Quota-Capped Laguerre Commitment ([`charts.py`](../../../particlegan/_t1_coupling/charts.py))**:
   - Maintains clock-free sufficient statistics $(S_k, N_k, V_k, M_{\text{fake},k})_{k=1}^K$ inside the checkpointed critic record (`schema=1`), with novelty-gated farthest-point insertion ($\min_k \|y - C_k\|_2 > 2.2\,r_{\text{near}}$, capped at $0.06\,d_{\max}$) so all $M=100$ modes of `grid100` accumulate across streaming $B_r=64$ batches without $O(K^2)$ Sinkhorn cost. Unvisited modes decay $(S_k, N_k, V_k)$ at the same $(K, B_r)$ rate $\alpha_{\text{eff}}$, keeping per-chart variance $r_k^2 = V_k / N_k$ exact when $n_{k,\text{batch}} = 0$.
   - **Topological & Host Screen**: Chart routing activates strictly when $K \ge 3$, topological $H_0$ persistence confirms disconnected valleys ($w_{\text{valley}}(1 - g_{\text{conn}}(Y)) > 10^{-4}$), and `Recipe.encoder_mode == "none"` (preserving explicit learned $E(x) \leftrightarrow z$ slot correspondences on AE/BiGAN/VAE tasks).
   - **Quota-Balanced Laguerre Monge Assignment & Intra-Mode Halo**: Solves $8$ log-domain Sinkhorn scaling iterations between uniform fake weights $1/B_f$ and deficit-boosted chart weights $\tilde{w}_k \propto w_k + 2[w_k - M_{\text{fake},k} - 1.60\,\text{SE}_k]_+$, rounds $\tilde{w}_k B_f$ to exact integer quotas, and assigns particles in descending order of Laguerre margin $\log \Pi_{i,(1)} - \log \Pi_{i,(2)}$ to **one** winning chart $C_{k^*(i)}$ (zero cross-valley barycentric averaging). Inside the strictly intra-mode halo $r_{\text{halo},k} = \min(\gamma_{\text{halo}} r_k,\, 0.35\,d_{\text{nn}}(C_k))$ ($\gamma_{\text{halo}} = 2.1$), settled non-surplus samples experience **identically zero** chart pull and receive an `absent_shield` suppressing false slack kicks whenever $n_{k,\text{batch}} = 0$ ($B_r < K$).
2. **Patch-Smoothed Conformal Multi-Scale Born-Infeld Horizon ([`primitive.py`](../../../particlegan/_t1_coupling/primitive.py))**:
   - Computes gap-guarded local real spacing $r_j \in \{d_j^{(1)}, d_j^{(2)}\}$ ($d_j^{(2)} \le 2.8\,d_j^{(1)} \land d_j^{(3)} \le 2.8\,d_j^{(2)}$), averages over intra-mode neighbors ($\le 3.2\,d_j^{(2)}$, $k_{\text{patch}} = \min(8, B_r)$) to suppress single-Gaussian Weibull tail fluctuations, and gates smoothly on real cross-mode width heterogeneity $Q_{85}(\bar{r}) / Q_{15}(\bar{r}) > 2.85$ via $s_{\text{raw},j} = (1 - g_{\text{width}})\ell_g + g_{\text{width}}\operatorname{clamp}(\ell_g \bar{r}_j / Q_{0.25}(\bar{r}),\, 0.70\ell_g,\, 4.50\ell_g)$.
   - Evaluates the smooth target-anchored Nadaraya-Watson field $\tilde{\ell}_Y(u) = \sum_j \operatorname{softmax}_j(-\|u - y_j\|_2^2 / (2(2.4\,s_{\text{raw},j})^2))\,s_{\text{raw},j}$ on any $u \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and forms the position-symmetric pairwise horizon:
     $$\ell(u, v) = \sqrt{\tilde{\ell}_Y(u)\,\tilde{\ell}_Y(v)}, \qquad c_{\text{BI}}(u, v) = \frac{\|u - v\|_2^2}{\sqrt{\ell(u,v)^2 + \|u - v\|_2^2} + \ell(u,v)}$$
     Because $\tilde{\ell}_Y$ depends only on $Y$ and evaluation coordinates, $X = Y \implies \ell_{XY} \equiv \ell_{XX}$, preserving **exact zero Feydy self-coupling bias** ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{OT}}(Y, Y) = 0$) while remaining exact identity $\ell_g$ on homogeneous distributions.
3. **Stateless Activation-Gram Whitened Generator Polar Step ([`deconflict.py`](../../../particlegan/_t1_coupling/deconflict.py))**:
   - Captures primary training activations $H \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d_{\text{in}}}$ on generator `nn.Linear` layers ($2 \le d_{\text{in}} \le 128$), computes regularized inverse square root $P = (\Sigma_H + \lambda \bar{\lambda} I)^{-1/2}$ via deterministic FP32 `eigh`, blends $\tilde{P} = \operatorname{rms\_norm}((1 - \beta_{\text{dec}}) I + \beta_{\text{dec}} P)$ with $\beta_{\text{dec}} = \gamma_{\text{dec}} g_{\text{disc}}$, and updates along $\operatorname{polar}(G_W \tilde{P})\tilde{P}$ with zero persistent state.
4. **Exchangeable Midpoint Ridge Conditional Drift Subtraction ([`primitive.py`](../../../particlegan/_t1_coupling/primitive.py))**:
   - On paired continuous conditioned fibers $(c_i, x_i), (c_i, y_i)$, fits ridge drift $\hat{B} = (\tilde{C}^\top \tilde{C} + \lambda_c I)^{-1}\tilde{C}^\top \tilde{M}$ on the pair-sum midpoint $m_i = \frac{1}{2}(x_i + y_i)$ so residual differences $(x_i - \hat{B}\tilde{c}_i) - (y_i - \hat{B}\tilde{c}_i) \equiv x_i - y_i$ are strictly symmetric under $x_i \leftrightarrow y_i$ (zero finite-sample bias at $q = p$) while preventing context drift from saturating diagonal-whitened IMQ conditional MMD kernels.
5. **Pure-FP32 Rationalized Costs, Deterministic `8x8` Spatial Pooling & $H_0$ Simplicial Early-Exit**:
   - Replaces FP64 transport GEMMs with scale-normalized FP32 rationalized Born-Infeld costs, projects $>8\times 8$ 4D images via exact reshape block-mean (`area_ratio = \sqrt{HW/64}`, avoiding non-deterministic `adaptive_avg_pool2d_backward_cuda`), and exits `simplicial_manifold_loss` before invoking $G_\theta$ whenever $H_0$ bottleneck persistence shows disconnected target modes ($g_{\text{conn}} \le 10^{-4}$).
