# Scale- & Distortion-Aware Moving Prototype Flow (`SMPF`)

`SMPF` (`critic_formulation="smpf"`, `prior_regularizer="smpf"`, `prior_reg=0.01`, preset `"smpf"`) is a clock-free, constant-learning-rate ParticleGAN formulation inspired by **s-means** (*Scale- & Distortion-Aware Clustering*, Kocabay, Yüksel & Yalçın, 2026; [`suayptalha.github.io/s-means-blog/`](https://suayptalha.github.io/s-means-blog/)) and building directly on ParticleGAN's *e-means*-inspired [`EMPF`](../empf/README.md).

## Core Algorithmic Pillars

1. **Clock-Free Online `s-means` Sufficient-Statistics Prototype Bank ([`update_smeans_prototypes`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/prototypes.py))**:
   Upgrades EMPF's 2-buffer `(S, N)` bank to the full $O(Kd)$ `s-means` sufficient-statistics bank `(proto_centroids, proto_usage, proto_sse, feat_scale, fast_sum, fast_count)` checkpointed bit-for-bit on the critic step record with zero RNG draws:
   - **4-Round Deterministic $D^{1.5}$ Coreset Seeding**: Replaces 1D projection initialization with deterministic multi-round $d_{\min}(x)^{1.5}$ seeding ($p = 1.5, R = 4$) so rare (2%) and off-axis modes receive initial prototype coverage on step 1 without outlier chasing.
   - **Tiled `addmm` Nearest-Prototype Assignment**: Computes exact L2 nearest-prototype assignments and residuals in cache-friendly tiles across all dimensions $d \ge 1$ (both vector mixtures and compact procedural images).
   - **Distortion $\times$ Usage$^{0.35}$ Anisotropic RMS Revival**: Replaces count-only splitting and fixed `split_eps = 1e-3` with `s-means` donor scoring $\text{sse}_k \cdot u_k^{0.35}$ and shift/scale-invariant anisotropic RMS perturbation $\Delta_s = 0.55 \sqrt{\frac{\text{sse}_s}{u_s d}} \, \mathbf{r}_s \odot \hat{\sigma}_{\text{feat}}$, allocating sub-prototypes to high-distortion stretched or broad clusters while preserving exact cluster mass shares.
   - **State-Settling Power-Cosine Rate & Empirical-Bayes Centroid Shrinkage ($\tau_{\mathrm{eb}} = 0.20$)**: Replaces epoch clocks with dimensionless transport unsettledness $u_{\mathrm{live}} = \operatorname{clip}(r_{\mathrm{ema}} / 0.16, 0, 1)$ and shrinks low-count fast pass means toward smoothed EMA centroids so rare clusters resist single-batch shot noise while abrupt shifts trigger instant turnover.

2. **Slack-Screened Physical Born-Infeld `s-means` Prototype Transport, Laguerre Outlier Pull & RMS Distortion Shell ([`SMPFGANLoss`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/loss.py), [`smeans_debiased_generator_loss`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/transport.py))**:
   Couples Empirical-Bayes prototypes $(C^{\mathrm{eb}}, w, \ell)$ directly to generator outputs $y = G(z)$ using Kantorovich-Hellinger slack-screened physical Born-Infeld ground costs $c_{\ell_k}(C_k^{\mathrm{eb}}, y_j) = \sqrt{\ell_k^2 + \|y_j - C_k^{\mathrm{eb}}\|_2^2} - \ell_k$ calibrated to each prototype's tracked `s-means` RMS quantization radius $\ell_k = \sqrt{\text{sse}_k / u_k}$, Laguerre power-diagram Monge assignment on valley stragglers (eliminating cross-ring origin cancellation), active-only entropic self-coupling debiasing (with discrete-codebook clone masking), and a per-prototype RMS distortion shell preventing covariance collapse on rare modes.

3. **Nadaraya-Watson Detrended Whitened Conditional Fiber Transport & Connected-Manifold Simplicial Alignment ([`smeans_conditional_fiber_loss`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/transport.py), [`smeans_simplicial_manifold_loss`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/transport.py))**:
   On continuous-conditioned critic fibers (e.g., DDGAN and AE-DDGAN reverse transitions), removes within-fiber conditional drift via vectorized Nadaraya-Watson kernel regression before evaluating coordinate-whitened multi-scale IMQ conditional discrepancy alongside row-paired relativistic log-odds (surviving `.detach()` via an instance-local storage capture ring), paired with coordinate-whitened AE reconstruction. On trainable zero-sigma `ParticlePrior` codebooks, aligns unseen latent $k$-NN chords, 2-simplex barycenters, and post-acquisition geodesic MDS charts onto $H_0$-connected `s-means` prototype manifolds without blurring disconnected modes.

4. **Impedance-Matched Thermal Settling & Coordinate-Anisotropic Prior Regularization ([`SMPFCriticPenalty`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/penalty.py), [`SMPFPriorRegularizer`](../../../particlegan/_smpf/prior.py))**:
   Matches state-driven thermal cooling across critic logits, EBI prototype-chord penalties, generator prototype/conditional/simplicial transport, and coordinate-whitened $k$-NN prior repulsion + RMS dead-particle revival tethers under one global constant-LR DualNorm recipe (`polar_cpu_v1`, batched quintic conv).
